# The Value Chain

**Loan Origination → Deconstruction → Construction** — closed into an *equity loop* by the
homeowner's choice to keep building.

This is the spine of everything ML Systems does. Each stage feeds the next, and the last
stage feeds back into the first: a completed rebuild creates equity, which qualifies the
homeowner for the next cycle. The loop is **client-driven** — it is not an assumption that
everyone loops, it is the observation that some homeowners *will choose to*, and the whole
system is built to make that choice rational.

> Reality labels used below: **MEASURED** (exists today) · **MODELED** (calibrated
> projection) · **ASPIRATIONAL** (a goal, not encoded in system logic).

---

## Stage 1 — Loan Origination: the Loan Pit

The real Node 1 is a **loan pit**: a reverse-auction marketplace where lenders and
capital-market participants **compete to fund the homeowner**. — *MODELED*

A homeowner alone has weak leverage with capital markets. In the pit, the relationship is
inverted: lenders bid to originate the loan, the homeowner reaches capital they couldn't
reach alone, and **ML Systems keeps the relationship and the data**.

- **Regulatory compliance** is an active workstream. The pit touches real lending, so
  licensing, disclosure, and state lending rules gate what can ship. *MODELED → MEASURED as
  counsel confirms.*
- **Stage-1 operations today:** the founder processes and manages originations; the pit's
  lender pool executes until in-house lending is operational. Lead capture routes to the
  lender pool while ML Systems retains the client relationship.

> **RCM (Reversed Conventional Mortgage) is PARKED.** It was an earlier product concept — a
> mortgage where 100% of the payment goes to principal and interest accrues as a separate
> deferred liability. The calculator/tooling still exist as app features, but the value-chain
> loop **no longer depends on it**. Node 1 is now the Loan Pit.

## Stage 2 — Deconstruction

The existing structure is taken apart, not demolished — so its materials survive.

- **2-day crane sequence** — *ASPIRATIONAL* (no ML Systems deconstruction has been performed yet)
- **80–90% material recovery rate** — *MODELED* (target)
- **RRR — Reuse › Resale › Recycle.** Full resale isn't possible, so the goal is to break each
  recovered material down to its **most valuable recoverable state**: reuse beats resale beats
  recycle. *(51% resale is a soft goal — a direction, not a metric the system enforces.)*

Deconstruction is where the material intelligence is generated: every recovered member is
identified, quantified, and valued. That inventory becomes both a salvage bank (which secures
financing) and a marketplace feed.

## Stage 3 — Construction

Recovered materials rebuild the home — larger than before.

- **+10% square footage** per cycle (footprint expansion) — *MODELED*
- **+1 level** added to the structure per cycle — *MODELED*
- **CONSTRUCTION_VALUE_MULTIPLIER = 1.43× per cycle** — *MODELED* (compounding, conservative)

Value is created by **physical improvement**, not market timing. See
[Ontological Compression](ontological-compression.md) for how the multiplier is calibrated.

> **AI operations efficiency (0–100)** is currently the orchestrator's *self-learning signal on
> our own runs* — the compression / floor-learning loop — **not** a live construction-site KPI.

## The loop, grounded

**Cycle 1** = one house on a **new foundation** (RI housing stock foundations are ~1960s and
often the limiting factor). **Later cycles expand on that same first home.** The flywheel is
simply the client choosing to keep building.

Cycle compounding at 1.43× (from a $500k RI home baseline):

```
$500k → $715k → $1,022k → $1,461k → $2,089k → $2,988k   (5 cycles)
```

## Why the loop is defensible

Every home that passes through the value chain produces a fully specified, ground-truth
**construction sequence** — the exact order, materials, and member specs of a real build.
That data is the highest-value product ML Systems makes, and it is the input to the
[Collective Ontology](collective-ontology.md) and, ultimately, to **ontology licensing** for
robotics — training data nobody else has because nobody else deconstructs and rebuilds the
same home across cycles.
