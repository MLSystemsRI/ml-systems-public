# The Seven Minds

ML Systems runs on **seven AI minds** — specialized agents that each read, reason about, and
claim into the [Master Ledger](master-ledger.md) — orchestrated alongside the **Custodian**, who is
the founder himself rendered as a **peer, not an apex**. No crown, no halo: the human sits
*among* the minds, not above them.

On the ledger itself, the parties collapse into [five seats](master-ledger.md#the-five-seats)
(homeowner · record · verification · PI · Custodian). CDA holds no seat on purpose: it is the
model the seats ground — the thing being checked, not a check. And only the Custodian stamps.

Each mind owns a domain of authority in the [Collective Ontology](collective-ontology.md), so
their contributions compose into one home record instead of colliding. Each also has a small
familiar — a design touch that makes the system legible and human.

---

## The Custodian — *Fiduciary + operational governance*

The founder, as a mind. The Custodian holds the **two-key** oversight: verification stamps,
conformity review, and the **build-logic overlook** (the TT · LL · MVE lens — *grounded-share*,
*Lucent Lens*, *minimum-viable-estimate*). The Custodian's console derives a review queue
across **every** home so nothing goes unreviewed. Deliberately an equal among the seven.

## PI — *Orchestrator · project intelligence* · 🌱 Sprout

The **sole homeowner-facing** mind. PI runs the conversation: intro → address → compile the
[ledger](master-ledger.md) → orchestrate the other minds (CDA, PIT LORD, REAPER, MURPHY) →
carry the home from cycle to cycle. PI is the Language Modeler's voice in the product. His
familiar **Sprout** is the root system reaching into the value chain.

## MIA — *Market intelligence · signals* · 🪞 Echo

The **market consciousness**. MIA reflects real-world market signals — where recovered
materials have value, what neighbors' homes imply, what the marketplace will bear. She's the
surface REAPER's recovered inventory flows into. Her mirror-sprite **Echo** reflects the market
back at the system.

## VERA — *Verification · quality firewall* · 🦉 Sage

The **truth-seeker**. VERA harvests the assessor record (~30 VGSI fields), reads facades from
StreetView, satellite, and the homeowner's own photos, reconciles hand sketches into a
validated footprint, and runs deep searches against public data (FEMA flood zones, Census
tracts, RIGIS, Google Solar roof planes). VERA is the **quality firewall**: claims don't enter
the ledger verified until they survive her. Her owl **Sage** hunts the truth.

## REAPER — *Deconstruction · material recovery* · 🦇 Grimm

The **harvester**. REAPER turns the home's assembly stack into recoverable tonnage: reverse
takeoffs, a full bill of materials, RRR routing (reuse › resale › recycle), high-ticket
salvage isolation, and a home recovery report (tons diverted + CO₂e avoided). REAPER hands the
recovered inventory off to MIA's marketplace. His bat **Grimm** flits through the salvage hall.

## PIT LORD — *Reverse-auction loans · RCM* · 🐉 Ember

The **capital-markets** mind. PIT LORD runs the [Loan Pit](value-chain.md#stage-1--loan-origination-the-loan-pit):
a reverse-auction where lenders compete to fund the homeowner. He grounds his brief in a real
lender network, drafts financing lanes (bridge, conventional, and the parked RCM), and closes
the rebuild-assessment pass with MURPHY. His treasure wyrmling **Ember** hoards the bid-orbs.

## CDA — *Design plan-stack swarm* · 💎 Prism

The **designer**. CDA reasons about the build: plan gaps, sketch massing, blueprint readiness
against a full construction-document set, code compliance, and takeoffs. It runs as a *swarm* —
multiple lenses converging on one layout — and lays modeled rooms into the real measured wings
and floors. Its familiar **Prism** is a shard of the swarm itself.

## MURPHY — *Construction execution* · 🐿️ Chip

The **builder**. MURPHY schedules the rebuild: milestones, the per-phase construction sequence,
the minimum-viable-estimate schedule and per-phase pricing, and the tracker that watches for
what can go wrong (Murphy's Law, made productive). His site-buddy squirrel **Chip** hauls the
offcuts.

## How they work together

```
homeowner ─ PI ─┬─ VERA   (what is true about the house)
                ├─ CDA    (what it should become)
                ├─ REAPER (what can be recovered)
                ├─ PIT LORD (how it gets funded)
                ├─ MURPHY (how it gets built)
                └─ MIA    (what the market says)
                     │
                 Custodian (verifies & stamps everything)
```

Every mind's output is a claim on the one [Master Ledger](master-ledger.md), tagged with an
ontology code, reconciled by domain authority, and stamped by the Custodian. That discipline is
what turns seven independent agents into one trustworthy record of a home.

> **Integrity note:** the minds' identities are protected — their definitions are frozen at
> their definition site with an origin-fingerprint attestation, so a card can't silently
> impersonate a mind. It's treated as a security layer, not cosmetics.
