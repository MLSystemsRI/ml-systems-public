# Neural-Net Architecture

ML Systems is organized around a **three-net ontology framework** — a way of thinking about the
company as three interlocking neural nets, each with three nodes. The framework, created by the
founder, governs how the [Collective Ontology](collective-ontology.md) communicates across
physical work, finance, and community. It's also the blueprint for eventual **humanoid-robot
synergy**: the physical net is what a robot would need to learn.

---

## Physical Neural Net — road map to critical thinking & problem solving

| Node | Full name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **ML1** | Manual Labor | Hands-on, physical execution |
| **ML2** | Measured Learning | Iterative learning from physical feedback |
| **ML3** | Machine Learning | Automated intelligence layer |

The progression ML1 → ML2 → ML3 is the arc from *doing* the work, to *learning* from it, to
*automating* it — and ultimately to a humanoid robot performing Tier-1 site labor. (The name
"ML Systems" lives here.)

## Dance Neural Net — US scaling priority & generational wealth

| Node | Full name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **EV** | Events / Sponsors | WSDC events; the attention & sponsorship engine |
| **CR** | Creativity | Brand differentiation through art and movement |
| **GW** | Generational Wealth | Long-term community value and legacy |

**Community:** West Coast Swing — the fastest-growing dance in the US.
**Strategy:** generate goodwill, attention, and value through the WCS community and WSDC events
to fund and scale ML Systems.

---

## Why three nets

The three nets map to the three things a durable company needs: the **ability to do the work**
(physical), the **ability to fund it** (financial), and the **ability to be known and trusted**
(community). The [Collective Ontology](collective-ontology.md) is the connective tissue — the
grammar that lets a fact discovered on a job site inform a financing decision and a community
story without losing its meaning.

Humanoid-robot synergy is the planned integration layer **across all three nets**: the physical
net supplies ground-truth training data (see [Ontological Compression](ontological-compression.md)),
the financial net funds the robots, and the community net supplies the narrative that makes it
legible.
