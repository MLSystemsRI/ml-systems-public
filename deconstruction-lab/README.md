# Deconstruction Lab

**Recover the materials, recover the intelligence.**

The Deconstruction Lab is ML Systems' R&D arm for taking buildings apart — the reverse-engineering
of assemblies that were built to stay together. It is Stage 2 of the
[value chain](../docs/value-chain.md), and it is where the company's material intelligence is
generated: every recovered member identified, graded, valued, and written down.

This folder is the public face of that work. It holds the method, the brief a writer works from,
and the articles that come out of it.

## The honest note

**ML Systems has not yet performed a deconstruction.** Everything in this folder is method —
designed sequences reasoned from framing practice, from the safety standards the work has to
satisfy, and from what materials are worth if they survive removal. Nothing here is a report from a
completed job.

The repo's [reality labels](../README.md#reality-labels) — **MEASURED**, **MODELED**,
**ASPIRATIONAL** — apply to every claim in this folder as they do everywhere else. Recovery targets
are described as designed-for, never as achieved.
